## Project Overview

AI Course CRM is a customer relationship management system for an organization that offers AI courses.

The system will manage potential students from their first expression of interest through follow-up, enrollment, and payment.

The CRM will also include a Machine Learning component that predicts the probability that a lead will purchase a course and explains the main factors behind the prediction.

## The Problem

Information about potential students can be scattered across forms, spreadsheets, emails, messages, and personal notes.

This makes it difficult for the course administrator or salesperson to:
- Track every lead
- Know the current status of each lead
- Follow up consistently
- Avoid losing potential registrations
- See how each course is performing

## Users

### Primary User
Course administrator / salesperson.

### Secondary User
Potential student interested in an AI course.

## User Stories

As a course administrator, I want to manage potential students and registrations in one CRM so that I can follow every lead and avoid losing registrations.

As a potential student, I want to register my interest in an AI course quickly so that the organization can contact me and complete the enrollment process.
